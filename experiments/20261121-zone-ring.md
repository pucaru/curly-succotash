## Hypothesis

The next safe-zone holding-point planner currently picks target positions on a ring at 52% of the next circle radius, capped at 88 world units. Choosing a tighter ring might reduce forced zone runs near final circles; choosing a wider ring might reduce exposure to concentrated FFA opponents. Both could trade off cover availability and squad survival.

Parameterize this scalar with `MECH_TUNE={"zone_ring_fraction":...}` and hold all other bot logic constant. Compare **0.35, 0.52 (exact production control), 0.68**.
