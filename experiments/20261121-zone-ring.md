# Zone hold-ring radius — controlled placement experiment (21 November 2026)

**Status: 432 matches audited, 9/9 comparison jobs plus aggregate successful; both candidates rejected.**

Base production commit: `60b5438af4d45af989f63bffcbe0b03250a23ae4`.
This is an isolated experimental branch; it does not change main or submitted history.

## Hypothesis

The next safe-zone holding-point planner currently picks target positions on a ring at 52% of the next circle radius, capped at 88 world units. Choosing a tighter ring might reduce forced zone runs near final circles; choosing a wider ring might reduce exposure to concentrated FFA opponents. Both could trade off cover availability and squad survival.

Parameterize this scalar with `MECH_TUNE={"zone_ring_fraction":...}` and hold all other bot logic constant. Compare **0.35, 0.52 (exact production control), 0.68**.

## Predeclared design

Use the independently implemented, MIT-licensed simulator at pinned commit `VivekRnx/bob-the-builder-2026@b76c216d8a7829e14409a42058eea93e46e9ccdb`. It is not the organizer's exact private engine.

Run **24 cooperative-squad seeds 711211–711234** and **24 one-hero FFA seeds 811211–811234** for every fraction × v1, v2, mixed independent opponents. 432 simulations total. A and B are paired by exact seed and opponent type. Exactly one tested bot occupies a FFA lobby with 15 independently fighting opponents.

The audit checks complete, unique seed sets, mean placement points, paired wins/losses/ties, early FFA deaths (≤2 points), FFA rear damage, and outside-zone seconds. The unit/conformance workflow must also pass.

**Strict promotion gate:** no negative mean placement delta in any opponent/mode, no increase in early FFA deaths and no increase in FFA outside-zone time, followed by a larger separate holdout with new seeds (minimum 64 FFA + 32 cooperative squad per population). Verify final-circle behavior and actual Docker/runtime conformance. These are screening, not a claim of statistically significant superiority.

Do not automatically promote even if screening passes. Preserve main, prior submitted commits and the previously cleaned history. Do not recreate the removed public-research section.

0.35 improves mixed FFA by +0.208 and cooperative v2/mixed by +0.167/+0.250, **but reduces v1 FFA by 0.500, v2 FFA by 0.167 and increases early v2 FFA deaths 2 → 3**. It also raises v2 FFA outside-zone exposure by +0.13s and mixed FFA rear damage by +101.8. **Reject.**

0.68 improves mixed FFA by +0.292 and v2 squad by +0.125, **but reduces v1/v2 FFA by 0.083/0.208, reduces mixed squad by 0.083, and increases mixed outside-zone time by +0.15s**. **Reject.**

The independent simulator measured a maximum FFA action time of 12.23ms; Docker/protocol conformance passed separately. These are local proxy measurements and do not validate private hidden-final performance.

**Decision:** no holdout, no merge to main, no official tournament submission. Preserve production `60b5438af4d45af989f63bffcbe0b03250a23ae4` and all prior submitted history. Since neither ring adjustment meets the cross-opponent gate, future trials should prioritize reducing rear damage or improving threat positioning rather than retuning the ring fraction.
